# P0.5 — Policy and Authorization

**Status:** Completed  
**Phase:** Phase 0 — Architecture and Security Specification

## Objective

Freeze the capability identifier model, default-deny policy semantics, contextual authorization, approval obligations, and operation-scoped authority required by Phase 1.

## Scope

P0.5 defines the distinction between capability support and permission, the deterministic destination-side policy model, trusted authorization context, interactive obligations, and the lifecycle of bounded authorized operations used by both control and data planes.

## Decisions and Constraints

- Policy evaluation is default-deny.
- Trust and capability advertisement never imply permission.
- Security-sensitive context is locally derived or cryptographically verified before it can influence authorization.
- Interactive approvals are evidence-bearing obligations, not boolean claims supplied by a peer.
- Successful authorization creates a bounded operation context rather than ambient peer authority.
- Authorized operations bind source, destination, capability/operation, session, lifetime/use limits, and relevant trust/policy revisions.
- Trust, policy, revocation, cancellation, or expiry changes invalidate affected authority.
- Phase 1 intentionally uses a small typed policy model rather than a generic policy language.

## Acceptance Criteria

The specification defines validated capability/version/operation identifiers, deterministic policy evaluation, effects and obligations, trusted authorization context, operation identifiers and bindings, expiry/use limits, revision binding, cancellation, invalidation, and data-stream authorization behavior.

## Verification

The policy model was reviewed against threat-model requirements for capability confusion, unauthorized operations, stale authority, malicious peers, stream misuse, interactive approval spoofing, and fail-closed behavior.

## Handoff

P0.5 provides the authorization contract consumed by the protocol, secure-session layer, Core Simulator, and every future capability implementation.
