# P0.4 — Pairing, Trust, and Revocation

**Status:** Completed  
**Phase:** Phase 0 — Architecture and Security Specification

## Objective

Freeze the Phase 1 pairing bootstrap, authenticated pairing transcript, explicit trust-record lifecycle, and revocation semantics without treating network proximity or transport identity as trust.

## Scope

P0.4 defines one-time pairing invitation state, transcript authentication, device proof of possession, owner-authorized membership, trust-record creation, revocation, reconnect behavior, and re-enrollment expectations.

## Decisions and Constraints

- Discovery, LAN proximity, BLE proximity, USB attachment, and transport identity do not establish Cross-Lab trust.
- Pairing binds owner/domain identity, both device identities and keys, protocol/profile identifiers, role direction, and fresh nonces into one authenticated transcript.
- Trust is committed only after explicit owner authorization and device proof of possession.
- Pairing invitations are one-time state and failed/cancelled/expired attempts do not leave ambient trust.
- Revocation invalidates ordinary future trust and must affect active/reconnected authority fail-closed.
- Recovery authority remains independent from ordinary trust.
- Low-entropy numeric pairing is not part of the v1 profile; adding it requires a separately reviewed PAKE or equivalent construction.
- The concrete v1 bootstrap/confirmation construction is governed by ADR-0003.

## Acceptance Criteria

The specification defines invitation creation/consumption, initiator/responder roles, transcript fields, confirmation and failure behavior, credential delivery, proof of possession, trust-record state, revocation authority, active-session reaction, reconnect-after-revocation behavior, and re-enrollment rules.

## Verification

The design was traced to the threat model for enrollment interception, identity substitution, replay, compromised peers, revocation, stale authority, and transport-identity confusion. It preserves default-deny behavior throughout partial or failed pairing.

## Handoff

P0.4 supplies the trust lifecycle used by policy authorization, session establishment, reconnect, revocation handling, and Phase 1 simulator scenarios.
